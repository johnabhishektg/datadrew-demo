# Research Synthesis (July 17, 2026)

Condensed from three research passes: (1) withsupafast.com portfolio — 9 live sites
dissected with raw CSS extraction; (2) Triple Whale + Polar teardowns; (3) Northbeam +
Ryze teardowns + 2025–26 trend scan. Full agent briefs preserved in session transcripts.

## The category's visual language (what everyone shares)
- Dark-first marketing (TW navy `#062940`/gold `#E6B455`; Polar anthracite `#16161C`/
  violet `#5433FB`/mint; Northbeam navy `#021230`/periwinkle `#7081ff`).
- Inter everywhere (TW: 821 declarations; 8 of 9 Supafast portfolio sites). Commodity
  set: Inter, DM Sans, Plus Jakarta, Space Grotesk, Manrope, Outfit, Satoshi; mono
  cliché: JetBrains/Space Mono.
- Dashboard-screenshot-in-frame heroes, GMV pricing sliders, Swiper carousels,
  logo marquees (You.com: 158 marquee refs; Astra: 78), stat count-ups, "AI-powered"
  headline framing, layered architecture diagrams.
- Same template skeleton: hero → logo wall → 3-step → feature grid → testimonial
  carousel → stat band → pricing → FAQ → CTA.

## What defines "great" right now
1. Tinted paper, not white; ink, not black (Onshore `#f7f5ef`/`--ink #11161d`;
   Kintsugi cream/pine; Uare lime-cream).
2. Three-voice type: characterful display + quiet body + mono for all numerals.
3. **Proof rendered as artifact, not claim** — Onshore's sealed tax manifest,
   Tradeify's payout card in the hero, You.com's live code blocks, Ryze's DOM-rendered
   dashboard (also crawlable by AI answer engines — GEO-relevant).
4. Named-number stat rows with specific (non-round) figures.
5. Workflow-as-narrative section order (sections named after verbs the product does).
6. "AI speed, human judgment" trust pairing for money-adjacent AI products.
7. De-risked dual CTA with quantified microcopy ("Get started in 3 minutes").
8. Motion budget concentrated in ONE signature moment; stillness reads premium
   (Onshore ships ~zero animation and feels the most expensive).

## Datadrew's opening (all three passes converged)
- **The ledger aesthetic**: paper + ink + editorial serif + tabular mono numerals +
  ink-green accent. Zero overlap with TW/Polar/Northbeam/Ryze palettes; argues the
  brand promise (honest, reconciled numbers) visually.
- **The product's output as the hero**: Drew writes diagnoses — show a typeset morning
  brief in real DOM, not a dashboard screenshot.
- **Transparency as design**: visible pricing (Polar demo-gates everything; TW demands
  12-month contracts), honesty boxes ("what we won't tell you"), reconciliation notes.
- **AEO detail worth stealing**: Supafast's pre-filled "ask ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity
  about us" deep links — directly relevant to our unbranded AI-visibility score of 1.1.
